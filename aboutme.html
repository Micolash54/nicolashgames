Hi there, I’m Nico.

I build strategy and management games where clear systems give players meaningful choices.

I’m a game developer with a strong foundation in Unity and C#. I enjoy creating games that blend clarity, complexity, and player agency, and systems that grow and reward thoughtful decision making.

My recent work is Bits and Boards, a game store simulator I’m developing solo. I design and implement its staff AI, customer simulation, economy, progression, UI, art, and performance work.

What’s in the box

Gameplay systems, AI behaviours, UI frameworks, full game loops, 2D and 3D assets, and the ability to carry a project from early idea through release. I work independently and collaborate across disciplines.
